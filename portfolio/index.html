FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website take?

Landing pages: 1 week. Full websites: 1-2 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start so there are no surprises.

What if I don't like the design?

Every project includes revision rounds (1 for landing pages, 2 for full sites). We work together until you're happy. I don't stop until it looks right.

Do I own the site?

100%. You get full editor access from day one. If you ever want to leave, everything is yours — domain, content, design files.

What about hosting and domains?

I handle all the technical setup. I can help you buy a domain, connect it, and set up hosting — included in the project price.

Can you maintain the site after launch?

Yes. I offer monthly maintenance retainers starting at $100/month for content updates, design tweaks, and technical support. Most clients stay on.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start, 50% on launch. I accept card, bank transfer, or PayPal. Payment plans available for larger projects.
